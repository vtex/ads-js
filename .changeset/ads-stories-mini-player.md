The Story card is now a button with a play icon in the middle (on hover or keyboard focus with a mouse, always on touch screens). Clicking it, or pressing Enter or Space, opens the same Story in a player at the bottom right of the page. Its expand button switches to an expanded view, centered over a dark backdrop (full screen on phones), and back. The player closes from its close button, with Escape, or by clicking the backdrop of the expanded view.
