Stories whose destination is a link (no product) show a call to action at the bottom of the card and the video: a glass pill with the text set in the campaign (the ad's `call_to_action`) and a caret, which opens the link in a new tab. On a card without the highlight, it moves the highlight, as the card does. A Story without the text shows no button.
