`label-unavailable` and `label-sold-out` replace the disabled button's text for a product that can't be bought and the sold out text shown with its price (and read by screen readers), as `label-add-to-cart` and `label-view-product` already do for their buttons. Missing or blank, each keeps the default in the element's language. They are also the `labelUnavailable` and `labelSoldOut` properties.
