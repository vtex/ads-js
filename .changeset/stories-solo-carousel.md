Show the Story as in the carousel with one Story: the card is centered over a 500px tall background as wide as the element's parent, with 24px around it (the card shrinks to 452px tall) and a soft shadow. On screens 576px wide or less, the card shows alone at its full 500px height, without the background.

The new `--vtex-ads-story-solo-background` CSS variable sets the background color (default `#fafafa`), and `--vtex-ads-story-radius` now rounds the background corners as well as the card's.
