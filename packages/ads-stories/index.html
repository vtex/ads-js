Default

Themed with CSS variables

Two Stories

Side by side: the highlight moves every 5 s (not while the pointer is over it), with the arrows, a swipe or a click on the other card. Only the highlighted card plays.

Two Stories, inactive autoplay

With inactive-autoplay, the other card also plays, muted.

Three Stories

The highlighted card in the middle, the others around it: every card moves one place per step.

Four Stories

Two cards on each side: the Story opposite the highlighted one shows at both ends.

Another language and currency

With lang="es-AR" and currency="ARS", the product prices read $ 129,90 instead of R$ 129,90.

Slow ad server

The response takes 3 s, longer than the default 2 s timeout, so nothing renders below.

Slow ad server, longer timeout

Same 3 s response, with timeout="5000": the card shows up after 3 s.

Product hydration

With account-name, the element asks Intelligent Search (mocked) for the products once the cards show. For 2 s the prices show a placeholder and the player's panel has no add to cart; then the fetched prices replace the ad server's (the first product drops from R$ 129,90 to R$ 99,90, the third gets a price it didn't have).

Product hydration, slow

Intelligent Search takes 8 s, longer than the default 5 s hydration-timeout: after 5 s the placeholders give way to the ad server's prices.

Product hydration, failing

Intelligent Search answers with an error after 1 s: the ad server's prices show, and the console logs the failure.

Product hydration, custom strategy

The hydration property set to the store's own fetcher, matcher and price reader (a fake catalog answering in 1.5 s): every product costs R$ 10,00 less than on the ad server, and the one without a price there costs R$ 50,00.

No Story

The placement comes back empty, so nothing renders below.
